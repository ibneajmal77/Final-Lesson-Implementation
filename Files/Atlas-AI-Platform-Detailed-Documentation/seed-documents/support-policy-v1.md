# Support Policy v1

## Refunds

Enterprise support plans have a 30 day refund window after purchase. The refund window starts on the purchase date shown on the customer invoice.

Refunds above 100 USD require manager approval before any payment tool can be executed. The support agent may draft a refund request, but the system must not issue the refund until approval is recorded.

## Citation Rule

When answering refund questions, cite this document and the Refunds section. Do not cite this section for account suspension or data-retention questions.

## Limits

This policy does not describe hardware warranty periods, account suspension rules, or long-term data retention.
