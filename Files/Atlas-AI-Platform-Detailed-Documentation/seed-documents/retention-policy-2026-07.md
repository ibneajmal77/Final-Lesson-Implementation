# Retention Policy Effective July 1, 2026

## Default Retention

For requests after July 1, 2026, Atlas uses the July 1, 2026 retention policy. The default retention period is 90 days unless a tenant-specific override exists.

Tenant overrides must be stored in tenant settings and must be visible in audit logs. If no override exists, the 90 day default applies.

## Older Policy Handling

The 2025 retention policy should not be used for new requests after July 1, 2026 unless a legal hold explicitly references that older policy.

## Citation Rule

When answering date-sensitive retention questions, cite this document and the Default Retention section.
