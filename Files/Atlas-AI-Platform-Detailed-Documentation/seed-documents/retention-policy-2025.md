# Retention Policy 2025

## Default Retention

For requests from January 1, 2025 through June 30, 2026, Atlas used a 180 day default retention period unless a tenant-specific override existed.

This policy is retired for new requests after July 1, 2026. Date-sensitive retrieval should prefer the 2026 retention policy for questions after July 1, 2026.

## Citation Rule

Cite this document only for historical retention questions inside the 2025 policy window.
